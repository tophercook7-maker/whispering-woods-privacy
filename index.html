🌲 The Whispering Woods

Privacy Policy

Effective July 17, 2026

The Whispering Woods does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information — from anyone, ever.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Network & data

The Whispering Woods runs entirely on your device and works fully offline. It does not connect to the internet, and no data ever leaves your Mac.

Children's privacy

The game is made to be safe and gentle for children. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it does not knowingly or unknowingly gather any information from children or adults.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
